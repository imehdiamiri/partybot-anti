How can we help?

Contact 8Playo support at info@digiget.com. Tell us which game or feature needs help, your device model, operating system, and app version if available.

Never send your password, verification codes, payment card details, or private voice recordings.

Email support

Games and recordings

Describe what happened and the steps that led to it. Reverse Singing recordings stay on your device unless you choose to share them. You do not need to attach a recording to report an audio problem.

Purchases and subscriptions

If you made an in-app purchase, check that you are using the same app account and Apple or Google store account. Try Restore Purchases in the app where available, or contact us with the product name and purchase date. Never send full payment details.

Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple App Store or Google Play subscriptions settings. Deleting an 8Playo account does not cancel a store subscription.

Account and privacy requests

You can request account deletion inside the app through Profile > Delete Account, or use our account deletion instructions if you cannot access the app.

For other data or privacy questions, email support. Our Privacy Policy explains data handling and advertising choices.
