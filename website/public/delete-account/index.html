Delete your 8Playo account

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data without installing the app again.

From the app

  1. Open 8Playo and go to Profile.
  2. Select Delete Account and confirm the request. Complete any sign-in verification requested.
  3. If an error appears, retry or contact support rather than assuming the account was deleted.

If you cannot access the app

Email info@digiget.com with the subject 8Playo account deletion request. Send it from the email associated with your 8Playo account and state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.

If you used a guest account or cannot access that email, explain this to support. We will need to verify ownership before deleting data. Never send your password, verification codes, or identity documents in the initial request.

Request account deletion

What happens to your data?

The request covers your 8Playo account and associated profile and gameplay data. Store transaction records and information required for legal obligations or abuse prevention may be retained as described in our Privacy Policy. Support will explain any applicable retention when handling your request.

Recordings and other files saved locally or shared outside 8Playo are not remotely erased by account deletion. Remove those copies from your device or the service where you shared them.

Account deletion does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel it separately through your store account's subscription settings.
