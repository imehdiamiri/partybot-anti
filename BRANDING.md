# 8Playo brand assets

The brand is typography-led: purple numeral **8**, white **Playo**, including the
final lowercase **o**. No robot, face, antenna or mascot appears in active brand art.
The app icon has an opaque indigo/blue background; Android's adaptive foreground
uses a transparent wordmark within the central safe area. Splash and header
wordmarks remain transparent. Onboarding keeps the friends and party theme.

Canonical sources are under `expo/assets/images/`. Web copies are in
`website/public/` and `expo/public/`; hashed exported copies are regenerated by
Expo export and `sync-web-build.js`. Onboarding page 3 keeps the existing live
name-entry form; the owner cancelled the badge redesign before publication.
Obsolete duplicate robot artwork under
`onboarding/logo on icon` was removed; Git checkpoints preserve recovery.

Assets were edited using the built-in imagegen tool, then resized/exported with
Expo image utilities. Current revision, October 17, 2026:

  icon background and hero confetti. Keep transparent backgrounds where present.

  favicons from these canonical images, preserving Android's central safe area.
