## Party Tools

Open Tools when your group needs a quick decision. Spin Bottle to pick a player, flip a coin for heads or tails, or put your own choices on the Wheel. Roll up to four dice, set the Hourglass for a round, or let Team Splitter shuffle everyone into teams.

Try it tonight: put three games on the Wheel and spin to pick your opener.

## Act and Talk

Act or Talk? Pick a deck in Tools → Ready to Use Cards.

Act: pull a Pantomime card and act it out silently. Can your friends guess “zombie in a shopping mall”?
Talk: read a question aloud and let everyone answer. Swipe when you’re ready for another.

## Four decks

Four decks for four moods.

Challenges: play one turn using only questions.
Penalty: deliver your next sentence like a movie trailer.
Most Likely To: everyone points to the friend who fits the card.
Couple: take turns answering a question about the two of you.

Find them in Tools → Ready to Use Cards. Play at 8playo.com
